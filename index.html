About

I'm Alex Kavallierou, a director and creator making commercials with generative AI. I handle the whole pipeline myself: concept, script, image and video generation, editing, sound, and the final 9:16 cut ready for Instagram, TikTok, and paid social.

I like ads that commit to a bit. A hydration brand can be a sword-and-sandals epic one week and a Street Fighter parody the next, and the product still lands. If you have a brand and a loose idea, I can turn it into a finished spot fast.

Based in Los Angeles. Available for spec work, freelance, and full-time roles.
